# Whiteboards

A whiteboard is an endless board to write and draw on with the pen, a finger or the mouse.
Switch on **Whiteboards** in Settings > Modules. Each board is a file, `<name>.board.json`,
beside your notes, so it syncs and backs up like any other file. Make one from a notebook's
menu (More to add > New whiteboard) or from the Whiteboards tool.

## Moving about

- Two fingers move and zoom the board. Once a pen has been used, one finger moves it too, so
  only the pen draws. A palm resting while the pen writes is ignored.
- With a mouse, the wheel zooms; hold Space or the middle button, or use the hand tool, to move.
- The small map shows the whole board and the part in view; press it to go there. The buttons
  beside it zoom, and show the whole board.

## Tools

- **Pens:** pen and pencil thin and thicken with pressure; a marker is even; a highlighter is
  wide and see-through. Each keeps its own colour and thickness. The star keeps the pen in use
  as a favourite, a press away in the toolbar; favourites are kept with the vault.
- **Tidy shapes:** hold the pen still at the end of a stroke and a rough box, ellipse or
  straight line is tidied.
- **Scribble out:** scribbling back and forth over things rubs them out, as elsewhere.
- **Eraser:** rubs out whatever it touches.
- **Shapes:** box, ellipse, line and arrow. A line or arrow begun or ended on something is
  joined to it, and follows when it moves.
- **Text and sticky notes:** press where it should go and type. Press twice to change it.
- **Images and PDFs:** the picture button, pasting, or dropping files adds images; a PDF adds
  its pages (the first 30) as images to write over. They are saved in an `attachments` folder
  beside the board.

## Selecting

With the select tool, draw a loop round things or press one. Then drag to move, drag a corner
to resize, or use the buttons above: recolour, copy, bring to the front, send to the back,
delete. On a keyboard: Delete, Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V, Ctrl+D to copy in place, Ctrl+A for
everything, the arrow keys to nudge, Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y to undo and redo.

## Not yet

Handwriting on a board is not turned into text or found by search, and boards are not
co-edited live: two devices changing one board at once keep the later save.
