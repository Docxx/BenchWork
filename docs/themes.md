# Theme atmosphere

Open **Settings > Appearance** to choose a theme, then adjust its colours, fonts,
page paper, spacing and widths. The gallery contains seven everyday themes and
thirteen fun themes. The current-theme panel previews the selected look.

The fun themes add vector scenes and small details around Home and the workspace.
Starship bridge has segmented consoles, Dino park has jungle gates and amber
signage, and Wizarding school has a candlelit castle above parchment cards.
The writing surface stays free of animated decoration.

## Atmosphere and sound

- **Show theme details** switches decorative framing and scenes on or off while
  keeping the selected colours and fonts.
- **Subtle motion** controls decorative animation and the opening animation.
  The operating system's reduced-motion setting takes precedence. Animation
  pauses while the app is hidden.
- **Timer sound** can match the theme, use classic beeps or gentle bells, or stay
  silent. Choosing another theme keeps this choice.
- **Timer volume** sets the alert volume independently of the theme. Zero is silent.
- **Preview sound** plays the current cue so volume can be checked before a timer.

Cues are original short sequences generated locally through Web Audio. They need
no downloads or internet connection. Theme families use console calls, enclosure
alerts, bells, orbital chimes, digital cues or sonar pings. Simultaneous timer
completions share one cue, but each timer still shows its completion message.

The first tap or keypress prepares audio on devices that require a gesture before
sound. A restored timer that finishes before any interaction may only show its
message. Device volume, autoplay and the operating system can also prevent audio;
sound is not a background alarm service when BenchWork is closed.

All choices are saved on this device. Existing settings gain the new controls
without losing their current theme or other customisation. **Reset to the theme's
own look** also resets the sound and motion choices.
